What You'll Find in the Patch

Our patch spans over 20 acres of field-grown pumpkins. Whether you need a giant pumpkin for carving or mini sugar pumpkins for baking, we have the perfect harvest waiting for you.

  • Classic Jack-O'-Lantern Pumpkins
  • White Ghost Pumpkins & Decorative Gourds
  • Sweet Sugar Pie Pumpkins
  • Fresh Cider, Cider Donuts, and Caramel Apples

Visit Us Today
