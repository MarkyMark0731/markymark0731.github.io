Our Story & Heritage

Founded in 1985 by the Miller family, Harvest Hollow began as a small family farm dedicated to sustainable agriculture. Over the past four decades, it has grown into a beloved community tradition for families seeking autumn memories.

Our Mission

We aim to bring communities closer to nature through fun, educational farm activities while preserving local agricultural heritage. Sustainability is at the heart of everything we grow!

Frequently Asked Questions

  1. Are pets allowed? Friendly dogs on leashes are always welcome in the outer patch area.
  2. Do you accept credit cards? Yes, all major cards and mobile payments are accepted.
  3. Is parking free? On-site parking is completely free for all guests.
