Things To Do at the Patch

From thrilling mazes to relaxing scenic rides, there is something fun for visitors of all ages!

10-Acre Corn Maze

Test your navigation skills in our annual themed corn maze. Can you solve the clues and find your way out?

  • Day Maze: Open daily during farm hours.
  • Flashlight Maze: Friday & Saturday nights from 7:00 PM to 10:00 PM.

Tractor Hayrides

Take a scenic ride around the farm property! Learn about farm history while taking in beautiful fall foliage.

Weekend Pumpkin Carving Workshops

Join master carvers every Saturday at 2:00 PM for expert tips on creating stunning Jack-O'-Lanterns. Pumpkin and tools are provided with registration!

Check out creative design ideas on Pinterest before attending!
